Aria2 Integration connects Safari to your Aria2 server, so you can send downloads to it from your iPhone, iPad or Mac in a tap or a click.

Right-click a link, share a link or a torrent file from any app, or paste links into the popup: Aria2 Integration sends them to your server, and you follow and control every download without leaving Safari.

SEND DOWNLOADS
• "Download with Aria2" in the context menu of links and selected text
• Share extension: send a link, magnet link or torrent file to Aria2 from any app
• Add links and magnet links from the popup
• Add torrent and metalink files from your device

FOLLOW AND CONTROL
• See active, waiting, paused, completed and failed downloads
• Progress, speed, time remaining and connections for each download
• Pause, resume, retry or remove a download
• Clear all finished downloads in one tap
• The number of active downloads is shown on the toolbar icon

YOUR SERVERS
• Add as many Aria2 servers as you need
• Secure connections and secret token support
• Default RPC parameters for each server, such as the download folder or a speed limit

PRIVATE BROWSING
• Use different RPC parameters for downloads sent from a private window
• Automatically clear finished downloads from the server while you browse privately

APPEARANCE
• Light, dark or automatic theme

REQUIREMENTS
Aria2 Integration controls an Aria2 server; it does not download files itself. You need Aria2 running with RPC enabled (for example aria2c --enable-rpc) and reachable from your device.

PRIVACY
Aria2 Integration only talks to the Aria2 servers you add. No account, no tracking.
